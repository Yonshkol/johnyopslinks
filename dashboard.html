Ops dashboard

Two minutes of setup

  1. Make a Google Sheet with one row per week and the columns listed below.
  2. File → Share → Publish to web → choose the sheet → Comma-separated values (.csv) → Publish.
  3. Copy the URL it gives you and paste it into dashboard.csv in config.js.

Columns, exactly these names in row 1:
week, yt_views, yt_subs, shorts_views, tiktok_views, ig_views, calls_booked, clients, mrr
week is a date like 2026-09-07 (the Monday). Blank cells are fine.
